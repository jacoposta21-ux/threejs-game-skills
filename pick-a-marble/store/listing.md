## Description
Build it. Pick it. Race it!

Pick a Marble is a 3D marble-run sandbox where every race is decided by real physics. Snap together straights, curves, steep drops, 360° loops, corkscrews, funnels, jumps, bounce pads, launcher cannons and moving obstacles, then pick the marble you think will win and watch the chaos unfold.

Every race is different: marbles collide, fly off jumps, spin around funnels and battle for the lead all the way to the finish line. Bet on the right marble and earn bonus coins!

FEATURES
- Powerful but easy track builder: 40 pieces that snap together automatically, with undo/redo, copy/paste, grouping and live track checks
- Real physics: momentum, collisions, loops, funnels, jumps and cannon launches
- Pick your marble: race 2–12 marbles and bet on your champion
- 12 challenges: piece limits, loops, jumps, multiple routes, speed and survival goals
- Random tracks: a new test-raced track at the press of a button, in easy, medium or hard
- Online rooms: invite friends with a code, each player races their own marble and scores points every round
- 50+ marble skins: sport balls, animals, food, planets, funny faces, metal, glass and neon
- 5 environments, level progression, unlockable pieces and skins
- 6 race cameras, including cinematic, follow and finish-line views
- Save, rename, duplicate, export and import your tracks
- Works on desktop and mobile

## How to play
1. Press PLAY to race on a featured track, or TRACK BUILDER to create your own.
2. In the builder, place a Starting Gate, then pick pieces from the library. Move them near a green arrow and they snap on. Finish with a Finish Line.
3. Press TEST RACE, choose how many marbles race and star the one you think will win.
4. Watch the race! Switch cameras, follow any marble, and see if your pick wins.
5. Earn XP and coins to unlock new pieces, skins and environments, then build something even crazier.

Online: open ONLINE, create a room and share the code with friends, or enter a friend's code to join.

## Controls
Mouse / keyboard
- Left-drag: rotate camera · Right-drag: pan · Mouse wheel: zoom
- Click a library piece, then click the track to place it (or drag it in)
- Click a piece to select it, drag to move, Shift+click to select several
- R: rotate · M: mirror · Q / E: raise / lower · Delete: remove
- Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: undo / redo · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V / Ctrl+D: copy / paste / duplicate
- Space: test race · 1–6: race cameras · Esc: pause

Touch
- One finger: rotate · Two fingers: pinch to zoom and pan
- Tap a piece in the library, tap the track to position it, then tap Place
