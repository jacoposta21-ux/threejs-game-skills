    TOO SLOW!

    The Slowest Racer. The Fastest Game.

    How to Play

    You are a snail. You cannot accelerate. Gravity can.

    Crawl

    W forward · AD turn · S brake / reverse

    Slow and grippy. Use it to line up a descent or recover. Refills slime.

    Roll

    SPACE tuck into your shell (and pop back out)

    Slopes make speed, momentum keeps it. The faster you go, the wider you turn. S drags the shell to brake.

    Slide

    Hold SHIFT to slime-slide

    Almost no friction: glide through mud, shallow slopes and long bends. Drains the slime meter.

    Boost & Tricks

    E shell boost when the meter is full

    In the air: Q / E spin. Land a full spin for points and boost charge. Land mid-spin and you lose speed.

    Stone is fast Wet leaves are fast but slippery Grass is steady Mud and water are slow
