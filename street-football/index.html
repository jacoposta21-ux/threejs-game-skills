HOW TO PLAY

LEFT HAND WASD move · SHIFT sprint  —  RIGHT HAND UIO over JKL · H   No mouse needed.
JShoot — hold to power up, release. Steer with W/S to aim for a post. Ball in the air near you: volley; back to goal: bicycle kick
KPass. Hold for a lob · with SHIFT a through ball · press again after your pass for a one-two
LSkill — with a direction (relative to where you face):
L stepover · L + side body feint (standing: ball roll · sprinting: quick cut) · L + back roulette (sprinting: heel flick) · L + forward nutmeg through a defender right in front (no one there: stepover-and-go · sprinting: rainbow flick) · L while charging a shot fake shot
IFlair — elastico · I + forward sombrero · I + back drag-back
UTackle — lunge for the ball; from behind is risky. With SHIFT: slide tackle
OSwitch to the teammate closest to the ball (control also follows the ball automatically)
HGamebreaker — style points fill the meter; fire it for 10 s of shots the keeper can't read
ESC / PPause. Arrow keys also move; the old keys still work (Space pass, E skill, R flair, F tackle, Q switch, G gamebreaker)

Every touch is a real kick: the ball runs ahead, checks, and your player's next stride plays it on in the stick direction — release the stick to stop it with the sole. Tricks score full points against a defender in front of you (a small showboat bonus when alone); skills play in a beat of slow motion and shield the ball from tackles; chain them for a combo multiplier (x2… x5) and score with a combo running for a STYLE BONUS. Failing a trick or losing the ball ends the combo. 3 v 3 plus keepers, 3 minutes. Win the ball back, keep it moving, and finish. Power shots fly but spray; placed shots near the posts beat the keeper more often. Touch screens get a stick and buttons.
