# Beyond the Hollow — game page text

## Title
Beyond the Hollow

## Short description (1–2 lines)
EN: A cinematic 2D adventure. Elias and Pip, his mechanical firefly, descend into the living darkness beneath Hollow Ridge to find his lost mother.
IT: Un'avventura 2D cinematografica. Elias e Pip, la sua lucciola meccanica, scendono nell'oscurità viva sotto Hollow Ridge per ritrovare sua madre.

## Description (EN)
Three summers after Hollow Ridge Station went silent, Elias follows his mother's trail down into the Hollow: a vast, breathing world of glowing mushroom forests, flooded ruins and forgotten cities. At his side is Pip, a tiny mechanical firefly who lights the way, wakes ancient mechanisms and learns new powers as the journey goes deeper.

- 4 chapters and 19 handcrafted areas, each chapter ending in a boss fight.
- Run, jump, climb, crawl, hide and shoot your Lumen Coil. Pip's boost gives you a double jump.
- Solve physics puzzles with crates, pressure plates, lifts and timed gates.
- Use Pip's Echo to switch between the present and the Keepers' golden past.
- Swing across chasms on the Light Thread.
- Protect your mother as she opens the Keeper doors.
- Face Crawlers, Stalkers, Burrowers, Guardians and stranger things that live in the dark.
- Find 50 hidden recordings. They tell the story, and they decide which of the two endings you get.
- Choose the boss difficulty (Story, Normal or Hard). Every boss attack is announced before it lands.
- Checkpoints and automatic saving.
- 6 languages: English, Italiano, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português.
- Keyboard, mouse, gamepad and touch controls.

## How to play / Controls (EN)
Keyboard and mouse:
- Move: A / D or ← / →
- Jump: Space (also Z, W or ↑). Press again in mid-air for Pip's boost (double jump).
- Climb ladders, vines and ledges: W / ↑
- Crouch, crawl, hide: S / ↓
- Drop through thin ledges: hold S / ↓, or press ↓ + Space
- Shoot the Lumen Coil: X / J / left click. Hold to charge a stronger blast.
- Send Pip to a mechanism, or to lure creatures: Q / K / right click
- Read a recording: walk into it, then E to continue
- Echo, to switch present and past (Chapter III+): R / U
- Light Thread grapple (Chapter IV): F / O. Swing with ← →, climb with ↑ ↓, let go with Jump.
- Pause: P

Gamepad:
- Move: left stick / D-pad
- Jump: A
- Shoot (hold to charge): X / RT
- Pip: Y / LT
- Interact: B
- Echo: RB
- Light Thread: LB
- Pause: Start

Touch:
- ◀ ▶ to move, ▲ ▼ to climb and crouch.
- On-screen buttons: JUMP, FIRE (hold to charge), PIP, USE, ECO, HOOK.

Tips:
- Pip's light keeps the dark creatures away: stay close to her.
- Watch for the golden ring on a boss. It marks the moment it can be hurt.
- Red markings on the floor show where an attack will land.

## Come si gioca / Comandi (IT)
Tastiera e mouse:
- Muoviti: A / D o ← / →
- Salta: Spazio (anche Z, W o ↑). Premi di nuovo in aria per la spinta di Pip (doppio salto).
- Sali su scale, rampicanti e sporgenze: W / ↑
- Abbassati, striscia, nasconditi: S / ↓
- Scendi dalle sporgenze sottili: tieni premuto S / ↓, oppure ↓ + Spazio
- Spara con la Bobina Lumen: X / J / clic sinistro. Tieni premuto per caricare un colpo più forte.
- Manda Pip a un meccanismo o ad attirare le creature: Q / K / clic destro
- Leggi una registrazione: passaci sopra, poi E per continuare
- Eco, per passare da presente a passato (dal Capitolo III): R / U
- Filo di Luce (Capitolo IV): F / O. Dondola con ← →, sali e scendi con ↑ ↓, lasciati andare con Salto.
- Pausa: P

Gamepad:
- Muoviti: levetta sinistra / croce direzionale
- Salta: A
- Spara (tieni premuto per caricare): X / RT
- Pip: Y / LT
- Interagisci: B
- Eco: RB
- Filo di Luce: LB
- Pausa: Start

Touch:
- ◀ ▶ per muoverti, ▲ ▼ per salire e abbassarti.
- Pulsanti a schermo: JUMP, FIRE (tieni premuto per caricare), PIP, USE, ECO, HOOK.

Consigli:
- La luce di Pip tiene lontane le creature del buio: restale vicino.
- Sul boss cerca l'anello dorato: indica il momento in cui puoi colpirlo.
- I segni rossi a terra mostrano dove arriverà un attacco.
