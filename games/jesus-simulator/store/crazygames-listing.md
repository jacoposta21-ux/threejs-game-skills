## Title
Jesus Simulator

## Short description (one line)
A funny, blocky open world in old Galilee: work miracles, help villagers, ride chariots and run from the Romans!

## Description
Welcome to Galilee! In **Jesus Simulator**, the holy open world experience, you play the carpenter's son from Nazareth on a sunny, blocky adventure full of miracles, missions and very grumpy Romans.

**Discover your miracles.** Explore landmarks to unlock six gifts:
- **Divine Push**: send fruit, pots and chickens flying.
- **Multiplication**: drown the market in bread and fish.
- **Water to Wine**: turn the town well red and start a party.
- **Walk on Water**: stride across the Sea of Galilee.
- **Healing Touch**: cure sore knees and twisted ankles.
- **Holy Levitation**: lift villagers into the air while they yell "PUT ME DOWN!"

**Explore an open world.** Roam Nazareth, the farms and Goat Hill, the fishers' hamlet, the Roman fort, the palm oasis, the caravan camp and the desert.

**Ride anything.** Hop on a donkey, a horse, a camel, a cart or a Roman chariot. Then set a lap record at the Little Circus.

**Take on missions.** Play The Wedding Wine, Fishers of Men, The Wandering Donkey, Bread for the Caravan, The Centurion's Servant and more.

**Gather followers.** Recruit followers who walk with you, earn Faith and spend it on Blessings that upgrade your miracles.

**Watch out for the legion!** Cause too much chaos and Roman Attention rises. Outrun patrols, hide from their line of sight, or get arrested and marched to the checkpoint.

Your progress is saved automatically. Every region has its own music.

## Controls
- **WASD**: move
- **Mouse**: look around (click the game to capture the mouse). The arrow keys also turn the camera.
- **Shift**: sprint
- **Space**: jump (hold to jump higher)
- **1–6**: select a miracle
- **Left click or Q**: cast the miracle (hold for Levitation and Multiplication)
- **Right click**: aim view
- **Mouse wheel**: camera distance
- **E**: talk / interact
- **F**: get on or off a ride
- **G**: followers wait / follow
- **Tab**: map
- **Esc**: pause menu

While riding:
- **W / S**: speed up / slow down and reverse
- **A / D**: steer
- **Shift**: gallop
- **Space**: hop

## Technical notes for the form
- **Platform:** desktop (keyboard and mouse). Mobile/touch is not supported yet.
- **Multiplayer:** no (single-player; the menu shows multiplayer as "coming soon").
- **Engine:** Three.js (WebGL) and Rapier physics, built with Vite.
- **SDK:** the game includes an adapter for the CrazyGames SDK v3. It handles loading events, gameplay start/stop, happytime, mute, data saving, and midgame and rewarded ads, but has not been tested against the live SDK. Test it with `?crazygames=1` and the CrazyGames QA tool before submitting.
